# Questions

Questions that we may wish to answer as part of the research.

 1. Who uses PHP? Are there any high-profile names?
 1. What PHP projects are the most popular?
 1. What version of PHP are in use? How can we sample this without missing groups, e.g. people who don't use Composer?
 1. What data sources can we use?
 1. Do we want to measure PHP in absolute or relative terms? If relative, how do we measure other languages which might not have widely available stats such as hosting or package installations?
 1. What are people using PHP for?
